How it works

It counts while you sit. It rests when you leave.

There is no schedule to set up. The bell starts when you sit down at the computer and starts fresh when you come back.

you sit downThe count begins

Your first bell is one full interval away: 60, 90 or 120 minutes, or any number you choose.

the time comesOne bell, one message

A soft chime and a single line. It fades on its own, or you click it away.

you step awayIt starts over

Five minutes without keyboard or mouse, or a sleeping laptop, ends the session. Missed bells are never replayed.

How it appears

Quiet card, soft overlay, or a plain notification.

Choose the one that suits how you work. All three ring the same bell.

Look at something that isn't a screen.

Card

A small card in the corner. Hover to keep reading, click to dismiss.

Let your shoulders fall a little.

Overlay

A translucent layer over every screen. One click or Esc and it fades away.

Little InterruptionsOne breath in. One breath out.

Notification

A normal system notification. It respects Do Not Disturb.

Good manners

It knows when not to ring.

A bell that interrupts the wrong moment is worse than no bell.

Smart pause

If a call, a video or a presentation is on, or your microphone is in use, the bell waits. It rings once when you are done, even if the call lasted hours. macOS only.

Welcome back

Return after a long break, three hours by default, and you get one short hello instead of a bell.

Quiet for a while

One click for an hour or three of silence, or skip just the next bell. The menu bar shows what is left.

Light and dark

It follows your system appearance, with smooth, slow motion that switches off if you prefer reduced motion.

Privacy

It stays on your computer.

The whole app is open source under the MIT license, so you can read exactly what it does.

  • AccountsNone. Nothing to sign up for.
  • Your dataSettings and progress are saved on your machine and nowhere else.
  • NetworkOne plain request a day for new messages from this project's GitHub. Nothing about you is sent, and you can turn it off.
  • MicrophoneSmart pause only asks the system whether a microphone is running. It never listens.
Get it

macOS first, Windows next.

The app is early. Until packaged downloads are published, you can build it yourself in a few minutes.

Open the repository
git clone https://github.com/itsspedram/Little-Interruptions
cd Little-Interruptions
pnpm install
pnpm build

Needs Node 22.18 or newer, pnpm, and Rust. Details are in the README.
